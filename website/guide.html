1. Getting around

ControlWhat it does
A touch or physicalPrevious domain page
C touch or physicalNext domain page
B touch or physicalLatch / clear 2ND
Function keys (12)Domain tools. Small 2 on a key means it has a secondary action
Number padDigits and + - * / = always available

Pages cycle: MATH → ELEC → MECH/RF → BUILD → KITCHEN → MONEY (scientific first). Header shows page name, optional 2ND badge, step tag, and Al wire mode.

On-device guide: latch 2ND, then tap the header (top bar). Scroll by tapping upper/lower half of the screen. Exit via the bottom bar, B, or CLR. Header shows hdr=HELP while 2ND is on.

Stamp a unit: type a number, press a unit key (e.g. VOLTS). That value is stored in a register. Solvers read set registers and compute the missing piece.

9. Worked examples

Example A — Ohm’s law & power

Find R and P for 120 V at 15 A.

1. Go to ELEC (A/C)
2. 120 → VOLTS
3. 15 → AMPS
4. OHM/PWR → 8 ohm
5. OHM/PWR again → 1800 W

Example B — Voltage drop & wire size

120 V circuit, 15 A, 75 ft one-way, copper. What’s the drop on 14 AWG? What gauge for ≤3%?

1. ELEC · ensure copper (header must not show Al; CONV 2ND toggles)
2. 120 VOLTS · 15 AMPS · 75 DIST
3. 14 AWG (optional stamp)
4. VDROP → ~7.07 V
5. VDROP → ~5.9%
6. VDROP → required AWG ≈ 10–11 for 3%

Example C — RC low-pass filter

R = 100 Ω, C = 1 µF. Find cutoff frequency.

1. 100 OHMS
2. 1 then 2ND FARAD (uF) — or enter 1e-6 and FARAD
3. FILT → fc ≈ 1591.5 Hz (τ also taped)

Example D — Mortgage payment

$100,000 at 5% for 30 years.

1. MONEY page
2. 100000 PRINC
3. 5 RATE%
4. 360 TERM
5. PMT → ≈ $536.82 (total interest also taped)

Example E — Roof pitch & rafter

Rise 12 ft, run 16 ft.

1. BUILD · 12 RISE · 16 RUN
2. SLOPE → 0.75 · again → ≈36.87° · again → 75%
3. ROOF → rafter length 20 ft

Example F — Room paint quantity

12×12 room, 8 ft ceiling, 1 door, 2 windows, 2 coats.

1. Perimeter = 48 → 48 RISE · 8 RUN
2. 1 DIST (doors) · 2 ANGLE (windows)
3. PAINT → net 333 ft²
4. 2 TOTAL (coats) · PAINT → ~1.90 gal (buy 2)

Example G — Baker’s hydration water

1000 g flour at 65% hydration.

1. KITCHEN · 1000 FLOUR · 65 HYDR%
2. 2ND HYDR% (or BAKE step) → 650 g water

Example H — Fraction from decimal

1. MATH · enter 0.375 · FRAC → tape 3/8
2. pi (2ND on num pad) · FRAC → tape 355/113

Example I — Running statistics

1. MATH · enter each sample · STAT after each
2. 2ND STAT → min, max, sum, mean
3. CLR clears stats with entry

10. Safety & limits

  • Wire sizing and voltage drop are calculator aids, not NEC compliance tools. Verify with local code.
  • Motor/prop numbers are first-order estimates.
  • Paint and concrete include simple waste factors where noted.
  • Firmware is built only on GitHub Actions. Install from this site’s USB button or download the merged .bin.
